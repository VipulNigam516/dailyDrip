Small, clean, and profitable

Companies under ₹1,000 crore in the cleanest two deciles of an Ambit-style accounting-hygiene check, profitable in the latest year. Some never lost money; others came back from losses. Each card shows ROCE, revenue, profit and EPS for up to ten years, with the year it turned from loss to profit marked.

Research tooling, not a recommendation. The hygiene decile is a filter for removing dark companies, not a quality score. Figures come from Screener's annual data and can be wrong or restated.

  • Who is here. Market cap under ₹1,000 Cr, hygiene decile 1 or 2 (cleanest fifth of the universe), profitable in the latest year, latest data not older than FY2025.
  • Sections. Years of profit since the last loss year, counted inside the last 10 fiscal years (FY2017-FY2026); a loss before FY2017 is not visible. Within each section companies are sorted by median ROCE over the years shown.
  • ROCE = (profit before tax + interest) / (equity + reserves + borrowings) at year end. Not computed when that capital is zero or negative, and it can look very high after losses have shrunk equity. Values above 80% are drawn at the top with a hollow marker; the tooltip has the true value.
  • Growth % is year-on-year. For profit and EPS it is shown only when the prior year was positive; a move from loss to profit has no percentage and is marked instead.
  • EPS = net profit / shares at each year end. Splits and bonus issues are not restated, so EPS and its growth are unreliable in a year where the share count changed (marked with a diamond; growth is blanked there).
