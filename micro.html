Small, clean, and profitable

Companies under ₹1,000 crore in the cleanest two deciles of an Ambit-style accounting-hygiene check, profitable in the latest year, with a median ROCE of at least 12% over the years shown. Some never lost money; others came back from losses. Each card shows ROCE, revenue and profit for up to ten years, with the year it turned from loss to profit marked.

Research tooling, not a recommendation. The hygiene decile is a filter for removing dark companies, not a quality score. Figures come from Screener's annual data and can be wrong or restated.

  • Who is here. Market cap under ₹1,000 Cr, hygiene decile 1 or 2 (cleanest fifth of the universe), profitable in the latest year, median ROCE of at least 12% over the years shown (companies whose ROCE cannot be computed are excluded), latest data not older than FY2025.
  • Sections. A turnaround is a company that had at least one loss year and has been profitable in every year since. Weak = 1 year of profit after the loss streak, mid = 2 consecutive years, strong = 3 or more consecutive years. Counted inside the last 10 fiscal years (FY2017-FY2026); a loss before FY2017 is not visible. Within each section companies are sorted by median ROCE over the years shown.
  • ROCE = (profit before tax + interest) / (equity + reserves + borrowings) at year end. Not computed when that capital is zero or negative, and it can look very high after losses have shrunk equity. Values above 80% are drawn at the top with a hollow marker; the tooltip has the true value.
  • Growth % is year-on-year. For profit it is shown only when the prior year was positive; a move from loss to profit has no percentage and is marked instead.
