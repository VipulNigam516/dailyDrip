Experimental, unvalidated. These metrics and scores are research tooling, not a recommendation and not investment advice. Figures come from Screener's annual data and can be wrong or restated. There are no industry labels yet, so every comparison is against the whole universe or a market-cap bucket, which mixes industries with very different normal margins and working capital. Treat anything surfaced here as a reading list to check, not a conclusion.
How to read this page
  • Every number is a percentile unless you pick "Raw values". A percentile of 80 means the company is better than 80% of the other ranked companies on that metric. Direction is already flipped where lower is better, so higher is always better in the percentile view.
  • Scores (0-100) are the average percentile of the metrics inside one idea, for example "Customers fund the business". 50 is typical, 75 or more is strong, 90 or more is very strong. A dash means too few of the group's metrics could be computed.
  • Breadth counts how many of the six ideas a company scores 75 or more on. Hygiene decile is an accounting-cleanliness check: 1 is the cleanest tenth of companies, 10 the least clean; 8-10 is flagged "dark".
  • Colour is the percentile: green is best, red is worst, in both views. A dash on a coloured cell means the metric could not be computed for that company.
  • Click the ? beside any column heading for what the metric means, how to read it and where typical companies sit. Click a company row for its 10-year picture and every metric at once.
